Common questions

How do I make a plan?
Open Discover, pick a country and city, choose your interests, budget and trip time, then tap to generate your plan.

Do I need an account?
You can explore without one. Sign in to save places and lists across devices.

A place looks wrong or closed.
Places, photos and hours come from Google. Tap the place to open it in Google Maps for the latest details, and tell us through "Send feedback".

Can I ask for a new country?
Yes. Open "Where should Jadwal go next?" in Discover or Profile and write any country. The most requested ones are added first.

What are tips?
Tips are optional and unlock nothing. Everything in Jadwal stays available without them. They help cover the AI and maps costs. Payments go through Apple.

A tip didn't go through or I want a refund.
Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?
Email 3wadhbamatraf@gmail.com from your account email and we'll delete it within 30 days.
