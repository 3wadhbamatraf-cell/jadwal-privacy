Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Jadwal ("we", "the app") is an AI trip planner for the Gulf and beyond, made by an independent developer in the UAE. This policy explains what information Jadwal uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: no ads, no selling your data, and no tracking you across other apps. We only use what we need to plan your trips and run your account.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and a user ID when you sign in.
  • Your plans and lists: places you save, your lists, your taste profile (interests), home city and default budget.
  • "About you" (optional): age range and gender, if you choose to share them. These help tailor suggestions and can be changed or cleared at any time in Profile.
  • Trip details you enter: city, dates or season, who is travelling, food needs and budget, used to create your plan.
  • Feedback and country suggestions: messages you send us and countries you ask us to add.
  • Purchases: if you leave an optional tip, Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

2. How we use it

  • To create personalised trip plans and show places, photos, maps and events.
  • To save your places and preferences across devices.
  • To answer feedback and decide which countries to add next.
  • To keep the app working, secure and improving.

3. Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account, saved places, feedback and suggestions.
  • OpenAI generates trip plans. We send the trip details and preferences needed for the plan, not your email or name.
  • Google Places & Maps provide place details, photos and maps.
  • Apple handles sign-in where used, App Store downloads and tip payments.

Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We only share what the services above need to run the app, or when required by law.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and its data, email us at 3wadhbamatraf@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

Jadwal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions? Email 3wadhbamatraf@gmail.com.
